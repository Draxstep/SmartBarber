## Brand & Style

### Personality & Direction
The design system bridges the tactile distinction of traditional barbershop heritage with the calibrated rhythm of modern operational tooling. Rooted in Colombian grooming culture, it takes cues from classic barber-pole striping, warm archival stationery, tailored sartorial linens, and early 20th-century print typography.

The interface evokes four core attributes:
- **Heritage Craftsmanship:** Grounded, authentic, and deliberate. Textural surfaces feel like heavyweight card stock and warm alabaster stone rather than sterile digital canvas.
- **Operational Exactitude:** Swift, unambiguous backoffice workflows designed for rapid scheduling, sharp financial tallies in Colombian Pesos (`COP`), and quick chair turnarounds.
- **Dignified Contrast:** High-contrast structural boundaries anchored by rich Carbon Black frameworks against warm cream surfaces, punctuated with measured Prussian Blue and Sangria Red accents.
- **Inclusive Accessibility:** Built for quick finger taps on mobile booking screens and dense high-efficiency scanning on backoffice tablets and point-of-sale registers, adhering strictly to WCAG 2.1 AA benchmarks.

### Design Movement
The design movement is **Editorial Heritage Minimalism** combined with **Print-Inspired Structured Layering**. It categorically avoids modern neon luminescence, translucent glassmorphism blurs, and floating organic blobs. Visual hierarchy is established through crisp geometric borders, dual-ruled typographic hairlines, generous negative space, and disciplined typographic scale.

## Colors

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Primary Canvas (`#F2EBDD`):** Warm Alabaster provides an inviting, non-glare foundation across both customer booking and operator views.
- **Card & Dialog Surfaces (`#FAF7F2`):** Warm Ivory creates soft optical separation without harsh pure-white clipping.
- **Parchment Accent Surface (`#E8DFCE`):** Applied to secondary table headers, disabled agenda intervals, and active row hovers.
- **Structural Anchors (`#1C1C1C`):** Carbon Black bounds navigation rails, public hero blocks, and modal overlays, granting weight and authority.

### Chromatic Brand Roles
- **Prussian Blue (`#1F3A5F`):** Operates as the primary operational hue. Directs focus toward active navigation items, confirmed appointments, form focus rings, and primary backoffice actions.
- **Sangria Red (`#8B1E1E`):** The signature customer conversion and alert color. Reserved for hero actions (*"Agendar Cita"*), active booking steppers, barber-pole hairlines, and promotional badges.
- **Carbon Black (`#1C1C1C`):** Functions as both the deepest neutral structural canvas and primary typographic ink.

### Contrast Directives & Dark-Surface Rules
- Deep Prussian Blue (`#1F3A5F`) and Sangria Red (`#8B1E1E`) must **never** be set as text or hairline icons directly against `#1C1C1C` surfaces.
- Over `#1C1C1C` dark surfaces, use derived tints: `accent-on-dark-blue` (`#7BA3D4`) for active states and `accent-on-dark-red` (`#E07A7A`) for badge counters.
- Base text over dark elements defaults strictly to `text-on-dark` (`#F2EBDD`) and `text-on-dark-muted` (`#BDB4A5`).
- Semantic badges must always maintain both tinted background tokens and accessible foreground tokens, paired with an icon.

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy & Roles
- **Editorial Voice (Playfair Display):** Reserved strictly for hero titles, section headlines, modal titles, and brand wordmarks. It establishes the traditional barber heritage. It must never be applied to buttons, table cells, or input labels.
- **Operational Neutral (Inter):** Serves as the UI backbone. Powers all button labels, forms, customer reviews, operational descriptions, and status pills to guarantee swift legibility at small sizes.
- **Data & Figures (JetBrains Mono):** Mandated for tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), booking verification codes (`SB-XXXXXX`), timestamps (`09:30 AM`), and Colombian Peso currency formatting (`$ 45.000 COP`).

### Formatting Directives
- **Currency Rules:** Always express prices as integer amounts with dot thousands separators followed by the COP currency descriptor (e.g., `$ 25.000 COP`). Never display decimal centavos.
- **Tracking & Proportion:** Headings in Playfair Display require subtle negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain editorial tightness. Small uppercase metadata tags must feature positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy & Grid Structures
The layout model employs a fluid column-based rhythm bound to a strict 4px micro-grid and 8px baseline spacing unit:
- **Mobile (`360px` – `767px`):** 4 columns with `16px` outer margins and `12px` gutters. Single-column stacked layouts dominate. Touch elements scale horizontally to fill safe container boundaries with zero lateral overflow.
- **Tablet (`768px` – `1023px`):** 8 columns with `24px` outer margins and `16px` gutters. Two-column service splits and responsive 2-column agenda cards.
- **Desktop (`>= 1024px`):** 12 columns with `32px` outer padding and `20px` gutters. Backoffice configurations anchor a fixed `240px` Carbon Black navigation sidebar alongside a fluid operational canvas capped at `1280px`.
- **Public Booking Flow:** Contained within a focused `max-width: 680px` centered channel to eliminate visual distractions and guarantee appointment completion in under 90 seconds.

### Operational Grid Spacing
The daily agenda planner operates on fixed 30-minute vertical increments:
- Each 30-minute block occupies an exact row height of `48px` on desktop and `56px` on mobile/touch interfaces.
- A 60-minute service spans two continuous blocks (`96px` / `112px`), maintaining strict vertical registration with sidebar time rulers.

## Elevation & Depth

### Surface Layering Philosophy
The design avoids diffused floating shadows and glossy blurs in favor of **tailored print layering**. Depth is established primarily via background tonality shifts and crisp 1px structural hairline borders (`border-subtle: #D5CBB8` and `border-strong: #8C8272`).

### Elevation Tiers
- **Elevation 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F2EBDD`. No shadow. Applied to main background viewports and page wrappers.
- **Elevation 1 (Cards, Tables, Panels):** Background `#FAF7F2` encased in `1px solid #D5CBB8`. Low-amplitude micro-shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.06)`. Used for service pickers, customer cards, and inventory tables.
- **Elevation 2 (Floating Action Bars & Popovers):** Background `#FAF7F2` or `#1C1C1C` with `1px solid #D5CBB8` and shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.10)`. Used for sticky mobile summary bars and dropdown menus.
- **Elevation 3 (Modals & Action Drawers):** Background `#FAF7F2` encased in `1px solid #8C8272` with deep shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.22)`, positioned over an explicit veil of `rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.55)`.

### Heritage Hairlines & Double Rules
To accent structural breaks, major section transitions on landing pages feature a heritage double-rule divider: a top `2px solid #1C1C1C` rule separated by a `3px` gap from a lower `1px solid #8B1E1E` hairline, evoking vintage barbershop parchment certificates.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Geometry
The interface uses a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric vocabulary. Corners are lightly refined to soften structural hairlines while avoiding playful or bulbous contours. The forms echo physical station card stock, ticket vouchers, and metal razor packaging.

### Radius Token Mapping
- **`4px` (Subtle):** Status tags, operational table checkboxes, 30-minute agenda time-block chips, and compact pill indicators.
- **`6px` (Component Standard):** Form text inputs, dropdown triggers, standard buttons, and time-slot selectors.
- **`8px` (Container Base):** Service cards, KPI summary metrics, inventory summary widgets, and notifications.
- **`12px` (Modal Standard):** Booking confirmation receipts (`SB-XXXXXX`), dialogue windows, and hero spotlight cards.
- **`9999px` (Complete Circle):** Barber profile avatar photos, numbered step progress circles, and live chair status dots. Rectangular elements must never use fully rounded pill geometry.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Touch Targets:** All clickable and tappable elements on mobile surfaces must measure `>= 44px` in height and width.
- **Primary Operational Button (`btn-primary`):** 
  - Background `#1F3A5F`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`, corner radius `6px`, font `Inter 600 14px`, minimum height `44px` (mobile) / `40px` (desktop).
  - Hover: `#162B47`. Active: scale `0.98`. Focus: `2px solid #1F3A5F` with `2px` offset.
- **Brand Conversion CTA (`btn-accent`):** 
  - Background `#8B1E1E`, text `#FFFFFF`, corner radius `6px`, font `Inter 600 15px`, minimum height `48px`.
  - Reserved for primary conversion funnels (*"Agendar Cita"* and *"Confirmar Reserva"*). Hover: `#6E1616`.
- **Secondary Outlined Button (`btn-secondary`):** 
  - Transparent background, border `1.5px solid #1F3A5F`, text `#1F3A5F`, corner radius `6px`. Hover: background `#E4ECF5`.
- **Destructive Button (`btn-danger`):** 
  - Background `#FCE8E6`, border `1px solid #B3261E`, text `#B3261E`, corner radius `6px`. Hover: background `#B3261E`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **On-Dark Button (`btn-on-dark`):** 
  - Background `#F2EBDD`, text `#1C1C1C`, corner radius `6px`, font `Inter 700 15px`. Used inside dark headers and sidebars.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Inputs:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #8C8272` (guaranteeing `3.56:1` contrast against ivory cards), radius `6px`, text `#1C1C1C`, placeholder `#6B645A`.
- **Focused State:** Border `1.5px solid #1F3A5F` with an outer ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(31, 58, 95, 0.16)`.
- **Error State:** Border `1.5px solid #B3261E`, accompanied by an explicit red warning icon and helper text below.
- **Time-Slot Selector Chip:** Minimum height `44px`, padding `10px 12px`, radius `6px`, font `JetBrains Mono 600 13px`. Available chips use `#FFFFFF` with `#8C8272` border; selected chips use `#1F3A5F` with `#FFFFFF` text; booked chips use `#E8DFCE` with strikethrough text and dashed border.

### Service Selection Cards & KPI Containers
- **Service Cards:** Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #D5CBB8`, radius `8px`, padding `16px`. Title in `Inter 600 15px #1C1C1C`, duration caption in `#4F4A42`, and price right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono 700 15px #1C1C1C`. When selected, container receives a `2px solid #1F3A5F` border and `#E4ECF5` tint.
- **KPI Metrics Card:** Background `#FAF7F2`, border `1px solid #D5CBB8`, radius `8px`, padding `20px`. Top label in uppercase `12px Inter 600 #6B645A`, primary tally in `28px Inter 700 #1C1C1C tabular-nums`.

### Status Badges & Alerts
- **Badge Rule:** Status must never be expressed by color alone. Every badge must combine a solid 12px vector icon with explicit descriptive text.
- **Badges:** Padding `4px 8px`, radius `4px`, font `Inter 600 12px`.
  - `Completada` / `Disponible`: Background `#E3EFE7`, text `#235D3A`.
  - `Pendiente` / `Stock bajo`: Background `#FDF1DC`, text `#8A5300`.
  - `Cancelada` / `Agotado`: Background `#FCE8E6`, text `#B3261E`.
  - `Confirmada`: Background `#E4ECF5`, text `#1F3A5F`.

### Colombian POS Quick-Select Payment Cards
- Cut registration modals present the 5 standard Colombian payment methods (`Efectivo`, `Transferencia bancaria`, `Tarjeta débito/crédito`, `Nequi`, `Daviplata`) as prominent touch cards (height `52px`, border `1px solid #8C8272`, radius `6px`) to ensure registration completes within 4 clicks.
